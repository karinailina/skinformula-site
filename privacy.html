SkinFormula

Privacy policy

Effective 14 September 2026

SkinFormula is designed so that your personal data stays on your phone. This page says exactly what the app stores, what it sends, and to whom.

What stays on your phone

  • Your answers from the questionnaire (age band, gender, climate, concern, goals, skin type, sensitivity, pregnancy status, products in use, budget).
  • Your scan results and self-assessment answers.
  • Your routine ticks, appointments, saved products, lab values and saved articles.

All of this is stored in the app's own storage on the device. There is no account and no server of ours. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The app has an "Erase everything" button in Settings that does the same.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Weather. If you allow location, the app rounds your position to about one kilometre and sends only those coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the ultraviolet index, temperature and humidity. Your position is not stored by the app beyond the current reading and is not linked to you.
  • Product lookup. If you scan a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Beauty Facts (openbeautyfacts.org), a public database, to fetch the ingredient list. No photo is sent.
  • Calendar and Reminders. If you choose to add an appointment to your iPhone Calendar or Reminders, the app writes that event into Apple's apps on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.

The app sends nothing else. It does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reporting services or tracking of any kind.

Camera

The camera is used for the skin scan and to read barcodes. Images are processed on the device. In the current version no photo is saved; a future version may let you save scan photos to a timeline, and those will also stay on the device.

Health information

Skin observations, pregnancy status and lab values you enter are health-related. They are stored only on your phone, are never transmitted, and are used only to build your plan. SkinFormula is not a medical device and does not diagnose.

Children

SkinFormula is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf: you control all of it on your device. Questions about this policy: hello@skinformula.app.

Changes

If a future version sends any further data anywhere, this page and the app will say so before that version is released.
