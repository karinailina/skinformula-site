Joining the test

SkinFormula is in testing on TestFlight. Testers receive an invitation by e-mail; install the TestFlight app from the App Store, open the invitation, and tap Install. Test builds expire after 90 days and are replaced by newer ones automatically.

Common questions

Where are my photos? On your phone only. Nothing is uploaded.

The plan changed after I edited my answers. That is by design: pregnancy, sensitivity, age and your concern change which nights get an active and at what strength. The reasons are listed under Treatments, Home care.

Numbers say "self-reported". Until the camera can measure, your answers set the numbers. They are labelled so nobody mistakes them for measurements.

Is this medical advice? No. SkinFormula is a wellbeing tool. See a doctor for anything that worries you.
