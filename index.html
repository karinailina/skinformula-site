Privacy

It stays on your phone

No account and no cloud. Photos, scores, answers, blood values, clinics and the chat live on the device. A backup is a file you save where you choose.

  • Your location, rounded, only for today's ultraviolet index, and only when you allow it.

Before you ask

Is the scan real, or a guess?

Real, and limited. The camera measures colour, shine, texture, pores and lines on the phone. It cannot measure hydration or elasticity, so those come from your answers and are labelled that way. A change under five points between scans is noise.

Does it diagnose anything?

No. It sorts findings into home care, professional cosmetology, a dermatologist, or a doctor soon, and it never names a disease.

Will it tell me which procedures to have?

It tells you what fits your age, skin type and scan, what is too early, and how often a treatment is usually repeated. The practitioner has the last word; the app helps you ask the right questions.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Russian: every screen and every article. The deeper knowledge base is being translated now.

What does it cost?

The scan, your routine, the treatments calendar, the weekly check and every article are free. SkinFormula Pro adds the personal skin assistant, skin analysis of your scan photo and product recognition from one photo: 9.99 a month or 59.99 a year (CHF 12.90 or CHF 79 in Switzerland), no trial, cancel any time in your iPhone settings. During testing everything is free.

Does the AI see my photos?

Only if you use a Pro feature and agree. Skin analysis sends one scan photo, once, and nothing is kept. Everything else stays on your phone. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.

What about the body, supplements and blood tests?

Tick what bothers you on your body, from back acne to eczema, and the routine covers it. Supplements are graded by the human evidence and checked against your medicines. Enter a blood result and the app says what doctors usually advise, which foods help, and puts the re-test in your calendar. Your doctor decides; the app never diagnoses.
