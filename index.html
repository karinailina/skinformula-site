Before you ask

Is the scan real, or a guess?

Real, and limited. The camera measures colour, shine, texture, pores and lines on the phone. It cannot measure hydration or elasticity, so those come from your answers and are labelled that way. A change under five points between scans is noise.

Does it diagnose anything?

No. It sorts findings into home care, professional cosmetology, a dermatologist, or a doctor soon, and it never names a disease.

Will it tell me which procedures to have?

It tells you what fits your age, skin type and scan, what is too early, and how often a treatment is usually repeated. The practitioner has the last word; the app helps you ask the right questions.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Russian. The knowledge-base articles are in English for now.

What does it cost?

During testing, everything is free. Later there will be one clearly named subscription, SkinFormula Pro. The scan, the routine, the calendar and the whole knowledge base stay free.
