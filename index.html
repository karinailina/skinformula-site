SkinFormula builds your routine from your answers and a scan: which nights get an active, at what strength, and what to skip. Every claim carries its evidence grade. "You already own enough" is a valid answer.

A plan from your answers

Pregnancy removes retinoids and strong acids. Reactive skin gets fewer active nights. Your concern picks the active. The reasons are shown, in plain words.

Honest about evidence

Forty-nine articles written against published guidelines and trials, each with its sources and a section on what is not established.

Clinic and home, one calendar

Book a course of peels, and the app pauses your retinoid around each visit. Day-by-day recovery timelines say what is normal and what means "call the clinic today".

Check a product

Scan a barcode or paste the ingredient list. The app says whether it suits your skin, which night it belongs to, and whether you already own something that does the same job.

Sun and air, live

Today's ultraviolet index and humidity for where you are, turned into one line of advice. Location rounded to a kilometre, never stored.

On your phone, only

No account. Photos, scores and answers never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes everything.

SkinFormula is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device. It never diagnoses. Anything that changes shape, colour or size, or worries you, belongs with a doctor.
